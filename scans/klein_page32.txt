1213+15042838, Nebula in Coma Berenices. Discovered by Méchain 15 Mar., 1781, and described as a starless nebula. Vogel observed it with the 8-inch refractor at Leipsic, and found it to be large, brilliant, and apparently resolvable at the centre, where there are two bright starlike points. With magnifying power 192, a spiral arrangement was visible.
1213+47592841, Nebula in Ursa Major, brilliant, with a nucleus, fairly large. Discovered by Herschel. No trace of resolvability. A continuous spectrum shows that this nebula is a very distinct star cluster.

0840+0651ε Hydrae (Σ 1273). A yellowish star, 3.5 mag., with a blueish companion 7.8 mag., distance 3.5″. Holden, with the great refractor at Washington, saw another very faint companion, d = 20″, p = 194°. [Schiaparelli finds the primary to be itself double, (1875—85).—Edit.]
0845+51461711, Nebula in Ursa Major, fairly brilliant, large, brighter at first gradually, then suddenly towards the centre, where is a star 10 mag.
0845+12151712, Star Cluster in Cancer, discovered by Messier, and described as a star cluster with nebula. Herschel resolved the whole into stars of from 8.5 to 12 or 13 mag. They number over 200. There does not seem to be any common centre to the cluster. A beautiful object!
0845+33531713, Nebula in Leo Minor. A very faint nebula followed by a star of 8.5 mag. Rather more brilliant towards the centre.
0845-064115, Hydrae. This star, 6 mag., has (as discovered by Herschel) a companion 7 mag., at distance 46″, angle of position 356°. Burnham divided the primary into two points of light at 0.5″ distance, and found an additional companion at distance 50″, angle of position 52°.
0847+0331S Hydrae, variable, discovered by Hind in 1848. The star is a yellowish-red. At maximum it is sometimes 7.5, though often only 8 mag.; at minimum it is under 12 mag. Period 256.4 days.
0847+3102ι² Cancri (Σ 1291). 6 mag., with a companion 6.7 mag., distance 1.4″.
0850-0841T Hydrae. Found to be variable by Hind in 1851. At maximum seems to be 7 mag., though often only 8 mag., at minimum it is under 12.5 mag. Period 289.4 days.
0851+4831ι Ursa Majoris, 3 mag., with a companion 10 mag., distance 10″, angle of position 357°.
0852+1219α Cancri. A star of 4 mag., with a very faint companion, distance 11.4″, angle of position 325°.
0854+324366, Cancri (Σ 1298). A white star of 6.5 mag., with a companion 8 mag., distance 4.6″.
0854+282367, Cancri. A star of 6.5 mag., with a remote satellite. It is at distance 103″, angle of position 323°.
0858+60591750, Nebula in Ursa Major, rather faint, but extensive. Rosse's telescope shows this object as a formless, nebulous mass of an uneven character.
0900+6737σ² Ursae Majoris (Σ 1306). 5 mag., and somewhat greenish, with a companion 8 mag., distance 2.6″, angle of position 245°.
0901+2328Σ 1311, Cancri. A star of 7 mag., with a companion of equal brilliancy, distance 7″.
0902+60321765, Nebula in Ursa Major, faint, fairly large, with a sudden increase of brightness towards the centre, with a kind of nucleus.
0908+69431781, Nebula in Ursa Major, fairly brilliant and large, with an increase of brightness towards the centre, where there is a star. Herschel thought it resolvable.
0908+0247θ Hydrae, 4 mag., with a companion 11 mag., distance 53″, angle of position 175°.

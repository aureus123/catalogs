1731-03104315, Star Cluster in Ophiuchus. A globular cluster, 7′ in diameter, easily seen as a circular nebula. Messier discovered it in 1764, and described it as a starless nebula, not large, faint, standing near a small star of 9 mag., easily seen with an ordinary 3½-foot telescope. W. Herschel resolved the nebula into stars with his 20-foot reflector. With a 4-inch refractor, evidence of its resolvability is apparent. It is surrounded on three sides by several brilliant stars.

0925+22021861, 1863, Nebula in Leo, large and brilliant, appearing as a double nebula in powerful telescopes; with a very strong instrument a third nebula is visible. According to Tempel, the whole is a large, spindle-shaped nebula, at least 15′ long, with 3 nodes, of which the southernmost is fainter than the northernmost, and the middle one is of a stellar nature and very brilliant. The spindle shape is ill-defined towards the north.
